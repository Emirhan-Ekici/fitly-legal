Fitly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Türkçe

In short

Fitly is an AI styling app for managing your wardrobe and outfits. You need an account; your wardrobe data is backed up in the cloud tied to that account and appears on every device you sign in on. On the free plan you can optionally watch a rewarded ad to earn a try-on; ads are served by Google AdMob. Premium subscribers see no ads. You manage the Premium subscription through the App Store or Google Play. To improve the app we use two more service providers: product analytics (PostHog) and error/crash reporting (Sentry); both can be turned off in Settings. You can delete all your data at any time from inside the app by deleting your account.

What we process

  • Email address
    Account creation and sign-in (Google, Apple, or email/password)
    Where stored: Supabase (EU — Frankfurt) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • User ID
    Identify your account, show only your data to you
    Where stored: Supabase (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Name (optional)
    Only when signing in with Google/Apple and only if the provider shares it
    Where stored: Supabase (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Wardrobe content: clothing photos, garment attributes, outfits, outfit calendar, style preferences
    Cross-device backup and sync
    Where stored: Supabase Postgres + Storage (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Body profile: gender, height, weight, body type, measurements (if entered)
    More accurate outfit suggestions; sync
    Where stored: Supabase (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Clothing photos — AI analysis
    Garment recognition and outfit suggestions. Sent through our HTTPS proxy to the Gemini model on Google Cloud Vertex AI for one-time analysis; not stored on our servers for analysis, never used for ads or profiling
    Where stored: Google Cloud Vertex AI (at processing time, not persisted) · Linked to you?: No
  • Body summary for outfit suggestions (optional, separate consent)
    Only if you explicitly allow it: a proportional summary derived from your body type and the measurements you entered (e.g. shoulder–hip balance, waist definition). Height, weight and raw measurements are not sent. Used to judge fit when ranking outfits
    Where stored: Google Cloud Vertex AI (at processing time, not persisted) · Linked to you?: No
  • Model try-on (optional)
    When you tap "Try on model", the outfit's piece photos and the model image (your personal model if you have one, otherwise a stock model) go through our HTTPS proxy to the Gemini image model on Vertex AI to create one image. The result is stored only on your device (and with the outfit if you save it)
    Where stored: Google Cloud Vertex AI (at processing time, not persisted) · Linked to you?: No
  • Profile photo and personal model (optional, separate consent)
    Your profile photo and your height, weight, body type and measurements are sent through our HTTPS proxy to the Gemini image model on Vertex AI to create a model image only for you. The generated model image is used for try-on. Your profile photo and model image are kept in Supabase Storage (EU) in a folder tied to your account
    Where stored: Google Cloud Vertex AI (at processing time, not persisted); Supabase (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Rewarded ad (optional)
    If you watch an ad to earn a try-on: a one-time record that the ad was completed and your account ID, so the credit can be granted. Google AdMob processes device and app information to show the ad. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking on iOS
    Where stored: Supabase (EU); Google AdMob (USA and other countries) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Subscription status (Premium)
    To unlock Premium features and restore purchases: product ID, expiry date and App Store / Google Play transaction ID. Payment card details are not sent to us; payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play
    Where stored: Supabase (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Quests, daily streak, invites
    Days you open the app, quest rewards, your invite code and who joined with it; used to grant try-on rewards. Inviters only see counts
    Where stored: Supabase (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Background removal (optional)
    If you choose "Remove background" when adding a piece, the background is removed on your device (iOS: Apple Vision, Android: Google ML Kit); the photo does not leave your device for this
    Where stored: On device · Linked to you?: —
  • Approximate location (optional)
    Weather-appropriate outfit suggestions only. Not stored; you can pick the weather manually instead
    Where stored: On device, transient · Linked to you?: No
  • Usage data (can be turned off): which features you use (e.g. opening the app, adding an item, generating/saving an outfit, an AI analysis failing), short labels (e.g. outfit occasion), app version, platform, language and account ID
    Understand which features are used and where problems occur, to improve the app. Contains no photos, item names/descriptions, free text, email or location; no session recording
    Where stored: Supabase (EU) and PostHog (US) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Error and crash data (can be turned off): error message and stack trace, app version, device model and OS version, current screen name, account ID
    Find and fix errors and crashes. No photos, screenshots, email or IP address are sent
    Where stored: Sentry (US) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • Feedback messages (optional): the text you write in Settings → Send feedback, its type (idea/bug/other), app version, platform, language and account ID; your email is used to reply only if you choose "You can reply to me by email"
    Read suggestions and bug reports to improve the app, and reply if you asked
    Where stored: Supabase (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes
  • AI usage log: for each AI call, your account ID, model, token count, duration and result code
    Monitor AI cost, prevent abuse and apply a daily usage limit. Request and response content (photos, text) is not stored
    Where stored: Supabase (EU) · Linked to you?: Yes

Turning off usage data: In the app, turn off Settings → Share usage data and that device stops sending usage events and error/crash reports (to Supabase, PostHog and Sentry alike). The AI usage log is required to operate the service safely and sustainably and cannot be turned off.

Ads and tracking

  • Free users see ads only as an optional rewarded ad: it opens when you choose to watch it, and if you watch it to the end you earn a try-on. A limited number of rewards is granted per day.
  • Premium subscribers see no ads.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission to personalize ads (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used and ads are shown without personalization.
  • EU, UK and similar regions: Google's consent form (consent to personalized ads) is shown; you can change your choice at any time.

What we do not do

  • Premium subscribers do not see ads; free users see ads only in the optional rewarded format described above.
  • We use no advertising SDK other than Google AdMob, and no tracking SDKs; no tracking across apps and websites. Usage and error data is shared only with the service providers below, solely to process it on our behalf.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.

Service providers (processors)

  • Supabase — authentication, database and file storage. Region: EU (Frankfurt). Your data is hosted in the EU.
  • Google Cloud Vertex AI (Google LLC) — one-time analysis of clothing photos you submit, generation of your personal model from your profile photo and your height, weight, body type and measurements (Gemini model), and try-on requests. The endpoint is global: processing may take place in the USA and other countries. Google Cloud terms apply; these requests are not used for model training.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — display and measurement of rewarded ads. See "Ads and tracking".
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — purchase, renewal and restoration of the Premium subscription. Only these stores process your payment information.
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc., USA) — product analytics: usage events, with your account ID. IP addresses are discarded; session recording and surveys are off.
  • Sentry (Functional Software Inc., USA) — error and crash reports. Reports are relayed through our own server; your IP address is not sent to Sentry.
  • Transfers abroad: PostHog and Sentry data is processed in the USA; Google AdMob and Vertex AI (global endpoint) data is processed in the USA and other countries. Apple and Google process subscription transactions on their own infrastructure. These transfers rely on the providers' data processing agreements with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs); the processing of your photos and body data with AI also relies on the explicit consent you give in the app.
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple — only if you choose that method, only for authentication.

Under Turkey's Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Article 11) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you can ask whether your data is processed, request information about the processing, its purpose and the recipients (domestic or abroad), request correction, erasure or restriction, object to processing and to automated decisions that affect you, claim compensation for damage, and withdraw consent at any time. Send requests to the address above; we respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your national data protection authority.

Retention and deletion

Your wardrobe data and account are kept until you delete them. In the app, Profile → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all cloud data (including photos, your profile photo and your personal model). Signing out keeps cloud data and clears the local copy on that device. Deleting your account also deletes your usage data; your account ID is removed from AI usage records, which remain only as anonymous statistics (model, token count, date). Feedback messages are deleted with your account. Your Premium subscription records (product ID, expiry date, transaction ID) are deleted with your account; you manage billing and renewal in the App Store / Google Play settings. Error reports in Sentry are kept for 30 days and usage events in PostHog for up to 12 months; when you delete your account you can also ask us to delete these records at the address below. The ad provider's own retention periods are governed by Google's policies.

Security

All network traffic is encrypted with TLS. The database enforces Row Level Security: each record can be read/written only by the account that created it. To understand how the app is used, the developer sees only aggregate counts (e.g. number of users, items per category) and the feedback messages you choose to send. For support and error investigation, the developer may view an account's photos (wardrobe, profile and model) only when needed; every view is logged and links are valid for 10 minutes. Item names and descriptions are not part of that view.

Children

Fitly is not directed to children under 13 (16 in the EU) and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Güvenlik

Tüm ağ trafiği TLS ile şifrelenir. Veritabanında satır düzeyi güvenlik (Row Level Security) uygulanır: her kayıt yalnızca onu oluşturan hesap tarafından okunabilir/yazılabilir. Uygulamanın geliştiricisi, uygulamanın nasıl kullanıldığını izlemek için yalnızca toplu sayılar (ör. kullanıcı sayısı, kategori başına parça sayısı) ve senin göndermeyi seçtiğin geri bildirim mesajlarını görür. Destek ve hata inceleme amacıyla geliştirici, bir hesabın fotoğraflarını (gardırop, profil ve model) yalnızca gerektiğinde görebilir; her görüntüleme kayıt altına alınır ve bağlantılar 10 dakika geçerlidir. Parça adları ve açıklamaları bu görünümde yer almaz.
